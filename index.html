About me

我是蔡昶宏 Henry，一名 5 年以上經驗的全端工程師。從電機工程背景自費轉職進軟體，這段經歷讓我習慣把陌生的問題拆解、學會、再做出來——這也是我最喜歡工作的部分。

這幾年在國泰世華與國泰證券，我參與兩大金融 App 從功能開發、資安檢測到上架的完整流程，也主導過核心功能改版與新技術導入（Jetpack Compose、Android 16 適配）。工作之外，我用 Kotlin Multiplatform、Spring Boot、Node.js 做自己的全端專案，前端到後端一手包辦。

我做事的方式是「解決問題導向」：先搞清楚真正要解的是什麼，再選對工具，然後穩穩地把它交付上線。我喜歡把複雜的東西做得清楚、把想法變成能用的產品。

I'm Henry Cai, a full-stack engineer with 5+ years of experience. I moved into software from an electrical-engineering background through a self-funded pivot — an experience that taught me to break down unfamiliar problems, learn fast, and ship. That's still the part of the job I enjoy most.

At Cathay United Bank and Cathay Securities, I've worked across two major fintech apps end to end — feature development, security review, and release — and led core revamps and new-tech adoption (Jetpack Compose, Android 16). Outside work I build my own full-stack projects with Kotlin Multiplatform, Spring Boot and Node.js — front to back, on my own.

I work in a problem-first way: understand what actually needs solving, pick the right tool, and deliver it to production reliably. I like making complex things clear and turning ideas into things people can use.
